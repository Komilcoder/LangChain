Business Trip Report: Samarkand, October 12-16, 2026
Section 3. Breakdown of Estimated Expenses

The total estimated expenditure for the five-day business trip for all four delegation members was $3,420.00 (approximately 43,776,000 UZS at a rate of 12,800 UZS per USD). Expenses were allocated across transportation, accommodation, daily subsistence, local ground transport, and business representation, in line with corporate travel policy.

Intercity transportation: round-trip Afrosiyob high-speed train tickets in Business Class between Tashkent and Samarkand for 4 delegates, at $85 per person. Total cost: $340.00.

Accommodation: 4 nights at a 4-star business hotel in central Samarkand, in 4 single standard rooms at $115 per night, including breakfast and high-speed Wi-Fi. That is $460 per person for 4 nights. Total cost: $1,840.00.

Daily per diem (meals and incidentals): the standard corporate daily allowance of $40 per person per day for 5 days, which is $200 per person. Total for 4 delegates: $800.00.

Local ground transportation: a rented corporate minivan with a dedicated driver for site visits across the industrial zone and the city center, at $50 per day for 4 days. This is a shared team expense. Total cost: $200.00.

Business representation and meetings: working dinners with key prospective partners, printed presentation materials, and meeting room rental. These costs were managed by Alisher Karimov and Malika Yusupova. Total cost: $240.00.

Total estimated expenditure for the full delegation: $3,420.00.
