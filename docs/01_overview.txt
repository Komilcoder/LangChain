Business Trip Report: Samarkand, October 12-16, 2026
Section 1. Executive Overview and Destination Details

From October 12 to October 16, 2026, a delegation of four specialists made an official five-day business trip from the central headquarters in Tashkent to Samarkand, Uzbekistan.

The main objective of the trip was to evaluate regional logistics infrastructure, audit local supply chain partners, negotiate long-term distribution agreements with regional manufacturing facilities, and oversee the preliminary setup of a new satellite operational hub in the Samarkand Free Economic Zone.

During the five working days, the team held eleven formal meetings with municipal representatives, logistics providers, and prospective corporate clients. These meetings established the foundation for the planned regional expansion in early 2027.
