Business Trip Report: Samarkand, October 12-16, 2026
Section 2. Delegation Members and Assigned Roles

The delegation consisted of four team members. Each was responsible for a different operational, financial, or technical part of the trip.

Alisher Karimov, Head of Regional Operations, was the delegation lead. He led high-level negotiations with regional government officials, oversaw strategic partnerships, and signed preliminary memoranda of understanding (MoUs).

Malika Yusupova, Senior Financial Analyst, carried out on-site financial due diligence on potential local vendors, evaluated regional tax incentives, and managed the travel budget and daily expense tracking for the whole delegation.

Jasur Rakhimov, Supply Chain and Logistics Coordinator, inspected warehouse facilities, assessed transport routes connecting Samarkand to neighboring regions, and negotiated freight rates with local transport companies.

Dildora Tursunova, Legal and Compliance Specialist, reviewed regional regulatory requirements, drafted commercial contract templates, and made sure all proposed partnership agreements complied with national corporate law.
